QUALITY CHECKPOINTS

Checks designed around the product—not a generic checklist.

The inspection plan varies by base product, decoration method, quantity and destination. The agreed scope can include:

AppearanceVisible scratches, contamination and obvious surface defects.
Crystal finishCoverage, color direction, pattern alignment and missing stones.
BrandingLogo position, artwork direction, labels and barcode information.
FunctionRelevant closures, lids, handles, wheels or compartments by item.
PackagingProduct protection, quantities, retail packing and carton condition.
EvidenceSample, production or pre-shipment photos by agreed milestone.
