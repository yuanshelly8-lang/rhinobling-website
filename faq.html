Frequently asked procurement questions

What is the MOQ for custom rhinestone products?

MOQ depends on the base product, rhinestone pattern, color assortment and packaging. Share the item and quantity to receive an item-specific MOQ.

Can I order a sample before bulk production?

Yes. A physical sample is recommended for custom designs so you can confirm the rhinestone effect, branding and packaging before bulk production.

Can you customize my logo, pattern and packaging?

Customization may include names, logos, artwork, rhinestone colors, product colors, labels, cards, barcodes and retail boxes, depending on the item.

How long do sampling and bulk production take?

Timing varies by item, pattern complexity and quantity. Your quotation will state the estimated sample and bulk-production schedule.

How do you control rhinestone quality?

The agreed sample and project specification are used as the production reference. Inspection can cover visible placement, color direction, branding and protective packaging.

Do you ship worldwide?

International shipping can be coordinated by express, air or sea according to order size, destination and deadline.

What information should I send for an accurate quote?

Send the product or reference image, estimated quantity, logo file, colors, packaging request, destination country and target delivery date.

Can you provide certificates or test reports?

Requirements vary by product and destination. Tell us the required standard before ordering so applicable documentation or testing can be assessed and quoted.

Can you copy a branded or copyrighted design?

We can discuss original artwork and authorized branding. Buyers are responsible for confirming they have the right to use submitted logos, characters and designs.

What happens if goods arrive damaged?

Report issues promptly with clear photos or video and order details. The response depends on the agreed specification, inspection scope and shipping terms.
