How to Reduce Rhinestones Falling Off Custom Products

Adhesion quality depends on the product surface, decoration process, curing, handling and inspection—not on a single adhesive claim.

Buyer-focused guidance from the RhinoBling export team. The correct test and care instructions vary by product.

1. Match the decoration method to the base surface

Stainless steel, coated plastic, fabric, rubber and glass behave differently. Before bulk production, confirm the exact base product and finish. A decoration process that works on a rigid tumbler may not be appropriate for flexible footwear or a frequently handled mirror.

2. Approve a physical sample

Use the approved sample as the reference for stone size, spacing, pattern alignment, color direction and visible adhesive. Check normal handling and product function before authorizing bulk production.

3. Protect curing and handling time

Freshly decorated products should not be packed or rubbed before the selected process is ready for handling. Ask the supplier how production scheduling protects curing, inspection and packaging.

4. Choose a production-friendly pattern

Very narrow gaps, sharp edges and high-contact areas can be more demanding. A good supplier should explain where artwork needs adjustment while preserving the intended look.

5. Define pre-shipment inspection

Agree what will be checked: missing stones, visible gaps, alignment, surface marks, residue, product function and packaging protection. Inspection criteria should reflect the item and approved sample.

6. Give realistic care instructions

Avoid unsupported claims such as “never falls off.” Provide item-specific care guidance, including cleaning method, heat exposure, soaking, impact and abrasion where relevant.
