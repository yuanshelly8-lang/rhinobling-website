Common questions

Before you request a quote

What is the MOQ?

MOQ depends on the base product, rhinestone pattern, color assortment and packaging. Send the item and quantity for an accurate answer.

Can I order a sample?

Yes. Physical samples are recommended for custom designs so you can confirm the rhinestone effect and branding before bulk production.

Can you add my logo or pattern?

Logo, initials, names, artwork, rhinestone colors and packaging can be discussed according to the product.

How long will production take?

Sampling and bulk lead time vary by item, design complexity and quantity. Your quotation will include the estimated schedule.

Can you provide custom packaging?

Options may include boxes, labels, cards, barcodes and protective packing, depending on the order.

Do you ship internationally?

International delivery can be coordinated by express, air or sea according to quantity, destination and deadline.
